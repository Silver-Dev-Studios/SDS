About

Silver Dev Studios (SDS) is the small team behind the projects on this site. We make profiles, Discord bots, trading tools, and little web experiences — each one hand-built.

Most profile and link-in-bio products chase the same playbook: maximize accounts, minimize personality. We think that is backwards. Our north star is identities.dev — a product where customization is not a premium tier, it is the point.

What we build tends to be small in scope, big on craft, and allergic to bloat. Expect more tools from SDS that share that philosophy: a handful of opinions, a lot of polish, and nothing that gets in the way of expressing who you are.

FAQ

What is Silver Dev Studios?
Silver Dev Studios (SDS) is a small independent studio that builds web tools, Discord bots, and experiences. Our flagship products are identities.dev, SDS Bot, Katanith, Spitari, and the SDS Code Editor.
What is identities.dev?
identities.dev is a link-in-bio platform that lets you build a profile page that actually feels like you. It offers more customization than Linktree, Carrd, or even Discord.
What is Katanith?
Katanith is a trading journal and analytics platform. It lets you log trades, protect your accounts with auto-lock rules, analyze your edge with advanced stats, and track the economic calendar — all in one clean workspace.
What is Spitari?
Spitari is a focused Windows interface for aim, visuals, movement, and player tools — featuring 2D/3D ESP, skeletons, raycast aimbot, RGB chams, and a target HUD. Access is granted through monthly or lifetime keys.
What is the SDS Code Editor?
A minimal, client-side code editor that runs entirely in your browser for Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Lua, HTML, and SQL. No servers, no uploads — everything stays local.
What can the SDS Bot do?
AI chat via Gemini, a full YouTube music player, a complete moderation suite, a ticket system with HTML transcripts, reaction-role button panels, custom embed building, and an advanced website crawler.
Is SDS a large team?
No — we are a deliberately small studio. Everything is hand-built, and we keep our scope tight so we can obsess over craft instead of bloat.
How can I contact SDS?
You can reach us through our Discord community or via the links on our site. We are most responsive in our Discord server.
